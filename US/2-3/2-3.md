# Story 2.3: Edit Product Listing

Status: ready-for-dev

## Story

As a seller,
I want to modify the information of one of my products,
so that I can correct errors or update prices and stock.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a seller clicks "Edit" on one of their products **When** the edit form loads **Then** all current values are pre-filled in the form

2. **Given** a seller modifies fields and saves **When** the form is submitted with valid data **Then** the product is updated and a success confirmation is shown

3. **Given** a seller tries to edit a product that belongs to another seller **When** the request is made **Then** the system returns an unauthorized error
