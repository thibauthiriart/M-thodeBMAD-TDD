# Story 4.1: Add to Cart

Status: ready-for-dev

## Story

As a buyer,
I want to add a product to my cart in one click from the detail page,
so that I can quickly build my order.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a logged-in buyer is on a product detail page with stock > 0 **When** they click "Add to cart" **Then** the product is added to their cart with quantity 1 **And** a visual feedback confirms the addition in under 500ms

2. **Given** a buyer adds a product already in their cart **When** they click "Add to cart" **Then** the quantity for that product is incremented by 1

3. **Given** a visitor (not logged in) clicks "Add to cart" **When** the action is triggered **Then** a message invites them to log in or create an account **And** the product URL is saved for post-registration redirect
